# 0009. The improvement engine follows the research spec

Date: 2026-11-21
Status: Accepted

## Context

The owner wants guaranteed improvement, but the evidence shows no training method is causally proven, and rating is very noisy.

## Decision

The 'Proposed improvement engine spec' in docs/research/improvement-engine.md is normative: 12 weaknesses with per-opportunity metrics, expected points lost as the common unit, at most 3 active weaknesses, an adherence gate, a 7-outcome decision table, a 90-day median baseline, and cohort experiments behind any claim.

## Alternatives considered

Promise rating gains; track rating only.

## Consequences

An honest, measurable promise: a rigorous process with a visible before/after for each weakness.

See docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-chess-improver-design.md.
