# Chess Improver: Build Roadmap

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-chess-improver-design.md` (normative, together with section 6 of `docs/research/improvement-engine.md`).

The design covers seven subsystems. Each milestone gets its own detailed plan, written once the milestone before it has landed, so each plan builds on real interfaces instead of guessed ones. Every milestone ends with working, tested software.

| # | Plan | Delivers | Spec sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `2026-11-21-01-foundation-and-analysis.md` | Monorepo, local infra, chess-core (win%, accuracy, judgement, PGN with clocks, phases, move records), Postgres schema, Lichess and chess.com importers, Stockfish pool, eval cache, BullMQ pipeline, CLI: username to analysed games | 6, 7, 8.1–8.3, 13 (partial) |
| 2 | `…-02-profile-engine.md` | Motif tagger, threat probe, episodes, weakness detectors W1–W12, Beta posteriors and priority, band reference builder (Lichess open DB), own rating-band opening explorer, repertoire tree and opening verdicts, local Syzygy, Maia service plus fallback difficulty, seven area scores, game_metrics, KPI snapshots, weakness timeline (change-point detection, states, onset dates) | 8, 8a, 8b, 12a, 12b, improvement-engine §6 |
| 3 | `…-03-ai-coaching.md` | `packages/ai`: report schema, prompts, structured outputs, claim validator, report and weekly-review jobs, Batch API, "Ask your coach" with tools | 9, 10a.9 |
| 4 | `…-04-web-foundation-and-design-system.md` | Next.js app, Better Auth (email, Google, Lichess OAuth), chess.com link and verify, DESIGN.md and ban list, tokens, `/styleguide`, board, eval graph and chart components, Playwright screenshot-critique loop, project design skills | 6, 11 |
| 5 | `…-05-product-surfaces.md` | Landing, onboarding with live analysis, report, seven area pages, game explorer, opening explorer, progress page, no-signup quick diagnosis, free single-game review (WASM Stockfish), `/licenses` | 10, 10a, 11, 12a, 14 |
| 6 | `…-06-training.md` | Lichess puzzle import, own-mistake puzzles, FSRS, opening trainer, endgame and tablebase drills, convert and defend vs engine or Maia, clock drills, safe/unsafe drills, bounded daily queue, forgiving streaks, lenient grading, per-sync retry moment | 10.5, 10a, improvement-engine §6.4–6.6 |
| 7 | `…-07-billing-sync-lifecycle.md` | Stripe Managed Payments and entitlements, scheduled sync, protocol re-analysis, adaptation loop jobs (weekly verdicts, EWMA drift), weekly digest email, monthly card and Climb Wrapped, share cards, age gate, export and delete | 12, 12a, 14, improvement-engine §6.6–6.8 |
| 8 | `…-08-hardening.md` | Docker images, Sentry, structured logs, Bull Board, cost metering and budgets, load test, security review, experiment framework, CI end-to-end, deploy runbook (no deploy without owner approval) | 13, 16, 18 |

Execution: subagent-driven. A fresh implementer and a fresh reviewer handle each task, and independent tasks run in parallel. The owner asked for parallel agents and continuous work.
