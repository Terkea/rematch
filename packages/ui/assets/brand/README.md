# Rematch logo

Drawn from a photo of the maintainer's dad as a young boy, holding his chess board, with his dad's hand on his shoulder (2026-11-23).

- `rematch-mark.svg`: the primary mark, the boy with his board and his dad's hand. It uses `currentColor`, so it takes the ink colour of wherever it sits (navy `#16233B` by day, pale ink `#E7ECF4` at night).
- `rematch-mark-mid.svg`: the same drawing with fewer, heavier strokes for 40-64 px (the headers and footer). From 48 px up the favicon, apple-touch and manifest tiles use it too.
- `rematch-icon.svg`: the mark as pixel art on a 32 px grid (whole-unit rectangles only), for the 32 px favicon: the boy, his 3x3 board (frame, a light ring, dark corners and centre, as in the mark) and his dad's forearm from the top-right with the hand on his shoulder, a clear gap round it so it doesn't read as the boy's own arm.
- `rematch-icon-16.svg`: the boy and his 3x3 board on a 16 px grid, for the browser tab (`icon.svg`, the site's `favicon.svg`) and the 16 px favicon, so it lands on whole pixels. Dad's hand is left out here, on purpose: in three rounds (a 1, 2 and 3 px forearm, the hand at neck and at shoulder height) a fresh critic read it every time as the boy raising his own arm, a stick or a wing, and it cost the boy his upright shape. Without it the tab shows a clear boy holding a board, like `rematch-mark-no-hand.svg`. The board has no room for the mark's light ring, and with 1 px squares the mark's dark corners close up into a ring round a dot, so here the corners are light and the dark squares touch corner to corner, which reads as a chequered board.
- `rematch-mark-no-hand.svg`: the boy on his own, kept for places where the hand doesn't fit.

The logo is ink only, with no red: in Rematch, red means dad's pen marking a mistake and nothing else.

The wordmark is "rematch" in Literata italic, the app's own serif.
