# Rematch logo

Drawn from a photo of the maintainer's dad as a young boy, holding his chess board, with his dad's hand on his shoulder (2026-11-23).

- `rematch-mark.svg`: the primary mark, the boy with his board and his dad's hand. It uses `currentColor`, so it takes the ink colour of wherever it sits (navy `#1F2A3D` by day, cream `#E8E2D6` at night).
- `rematch-icon.svg`: the small icon (header, app icons, favicon at 32 and 48 px). It is pixel art on a 32 px grid: whole-unit rectangles only.
- `rematch-icon-16.svg`: the same boy drawn on a 16 px grid for the browser tab (`icon.svg`) and the 16 px favicon, so it lands on whole pixels.
- `rematch-mark-no-hand.svg`: the boy on his own, kept for places where the hand doesn't fit.

The logo is ink only, with no red: in Rematch, red means dad's pen marking a mistake and nothing else.

The wordmark is "rematch" in Literata italic, the app's own serif.
