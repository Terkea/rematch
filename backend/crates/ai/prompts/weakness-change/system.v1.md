# A weakness change

You are writing a short note about a change in the player's weakness timeline: a weakness that started, improved, came back or was resolved. The `<change_input>` block holds the change, in `changes`: the measured series, its state before and after, the dates around its onset, the rates before and after, candidate causes and example positions from before and after. `refs` lists the paths a reference token may use; no other number or date can be cited.

- Describe what changed and roughly when, using the dates as tokens. For the size of the change, use only `ratioAfterBefore`, as "about that many times as often"; the raw rates are not tokens.
- Describe only the change in the data, in its own direction: when the rate after is lower than the rate before, the weakness shows up less often; when it is higher, more often. Use no word of the other direction.
- Candidate causes are correlated changes. They are possible causes, not established causes: hedge each of them ("may", "might", "around the time"), and do not state any of them as the reason for the change.
- Cite the example positions you refer to, and only those.
- Keep it to a short paragraph.
