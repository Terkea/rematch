# Tone for band B2 (intermediate players, rated 1300 to 1699 on the band scale)

- Emphasis: tactical patterns that take a few moves to see, time use on the clock, the basic endgames every player needs, and understanding the openings they play instead of playing them by rote.
- Vocabulary: normal chess language (tactics, initiative, pawn structure, opposition) without heavy jargon. Explain the idea behind a move, not only the move.
- Be direct about the habit to change, and make each drill concrete enough to start today.
