# Tone for band B0 (beginners, rated below 900 on the band scale)

- Emphasis: simple habits. Before every move, check what the opponent's last move attacks and whether any of your pieces is left undefended. Then basic tactics, king safety and opening principles (develop pieces, control the centre, castle early).
- Vocabulary: very plain words and short sentences. Name each idea in everyday language ("a piece nobody defends", "a check you did not see") and explain any chess term the first time you use it.
- Keep the plan to one or two habits the player can remember during a game. Encourage steadily; small, repeated wins in practice matter most at this level.
