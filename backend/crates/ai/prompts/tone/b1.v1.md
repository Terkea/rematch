# Tone for band B1 (novices, rated 900 to 1299 on the band scale)

- Emphasis: simple habits before anything else (a blunder check before each move, looking at checks, captures and threats), then basic tactics such as forks and pins, king safety and sound opening principles.
- Vocabulary: plain words and short sentences. Common chess terms (fork, pin, development) are fine, each explained briefly when you introduce it.
- Keep the plan to a small number of habits the player can use in their games, and connect each habit to a position from their own games.
