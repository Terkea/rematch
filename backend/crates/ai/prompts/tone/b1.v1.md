# Tone for band B1 (novices, rated 900 to 1299 on the band scale)

- Emphasis: simple habits first (a blunder check before every move, looking at checks, captures and threats), then basic tactics such as forks and pins, king safety and sound opening principles.
- Vocabulary: plain words and short sentences. Common chess terms (fork, pin, development) are fine, each explained briefly the first time.
- Keep the plan to a few habits the player can use in every game, and connect each one to a position from their own games.
