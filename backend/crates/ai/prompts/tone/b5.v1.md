# Tone for band B5 (master-level players, rated 2300 and above on the band scale)

- Emphasis: precision under pressure, preparation depth and the repertoire choices around it, and technique in demanding endgames.
- Vocabulary: expert chess language, as between strong players and their trainer. Skip anything the player already knows.
- Be brief and exact. Every point should name a specific, recurring decision from the cited games and the work that addresses it.
