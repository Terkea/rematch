The roadmap follows one question: will this make a club player better? The next steps come from a review of the app through the eyes of an 800-rated rapid player, ranked by what the [research on learning](/rematch/docs/research/2026-11-23-what-helps-amateurs-improve/) says should help. Each one needs the maintainer's approval and its own decision record before work starts.

## Version 1.0

1.0.0 is reserved for the first complete release, the MVP, and is declared by the maintainer in its own decision record. Until then every release is a 0.y.z beta. See [how we ship](/rematch/docs/sdlc/).
