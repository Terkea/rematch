What this is

You made a move that let your opponent win material straight away.

How it's measured

Chances: your moves in a position that was still open (your win% above 10 and below 90). It counts when your move lost 15 win% points or more and your opponent's best reply wins at least 2 pawns' worth of material within 3 half-moves, without a mate. A blunder whose refutation the engine did not record is left out.

Why it matters

Free material is the most common way games are decided at club level, and it is the easiest leak to plug.
