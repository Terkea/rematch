# EXP-0007: review

## Part one: the pre-registration, before the run

A fresh critic (an agent with the `scientific-critical-thinking` and `peer-review` skills, which had not worked on this experiment) read the pre-registration of commit 378a84fe, `analysis.py`, the pilot, the hand check, the power analysis and the bridge. It checked that `sample.csv` and `sample-pilot.csv` share no id (23,817 against 1,874 then, none shared), that the checksums matched, and that the tagger last changed on 2026-11-21, before the pilot. Its 13 concerns, and what was done about each, in the commit after 3e7a805b:

1. **The themes cannot decide "hide" for label-limited tags** (sections 2 and 9). The pilot's precisions for `pin`, `hangingPiece`, `sacrifice`, `backRankMate` and `skewer` were already near or below 0.80, and most of their checked false positives were label gaps, so "hidden" was close to fixed in advance on a metric we call a lower bound. Must fix. Done: section 9 now has a fourth branch, "hidden pending a human audit", for the six label-limited tags (fixed in `LABEL_LIMITED` before the run). A precision below the floor hides such a tag as a precaution, not as a finding that it is wrong, and the decision passes to a blind audit by a person, filed as a follow-up with its own value case. We did not register the audit inside this experiment: the protocol keeps an LLM's judgement out of any decision (`docs/evaluation.md`), and a person's audit of a few hundred positions is a cost of its own that needs its own value case.
2. **The hand check is unblinded, small, and judged on our definitions** (section 7). Should fix. Done in part: section 7 now says the verdicts were made by Claude, not by a person, and not blind to our tags; it gives the pooled share of label gaps with its interval (19 of 26, 0.73, 95% Wilson 0.54 to 0.86) and says that the check decides only which branch a hidden tag takes. A "label error" verdict was already used (two `backRankMate` puzzles with no mate in the solution). Not changed: the critic read our `backRankMate` rule as any rook or queen mate on the back rank; `rules.rs` (`back_rank_mate`) also requires every forward square of the king to hold one of its own pieces, which is Lichess's definition, so the four verdicts stand. The check was not redone blind: a blind redo by the same agent would still be an LLM's.
3. **Type I error not checked where it matters** (sections 6 and 8). Must fix. Done: the power analysis now simulates the false-show rate at true precisions 0.80, 0.75 and 0.70 and the coverage at 0.90 for Kish's effective sample size and for the stratified, linearised ratio variance, at every design. Kish's under-covers (false-show rate at 0.80 up to 0.063 for `smotheredMate` at m = 300, f = 0.01, against a nominal 0.0008); the linearised variance holds at every design with f of at least 0.005. Section 8 now uses the linearised variance. With it the smallest design that reaches 0.80 for every tag is m = 50, f = 0.03, so the confirmatory sample was redrawn at that size (54,987 puzzles, from the same seed), before any confirmatory tag was seen.
4. **Design choices changed after the pilot, undeclared** (sections 6 and 7). Should fix. Done: section 7 lists every change made after the pilot and before the run (the allocation, the grid, the selection rule, the added checks, the variance, the fourth branch, the mate-length reporting, the exclusion limit, the disjointness check), and this review lists them too. The floors, minimum effects, mapping and the three branches of commit b7cc116a are unchanged.
5. **Puzzle lines are not production lines** (section 2). Should fix. Done in part: every claim is now restricted to the tags of a puzzle's solution line, and the check the critic proposed (tagging **Stockfish 19**'s own best line at the production settings on a seeded sub-sample) is filed under "Next" as a follow-up experiment. Not done here: it needs the engine inside the bridge (`cli lab tag-motifs` is pure today) and an engine-heavy run, which the lane rules allow one at a time across lanes.
6. **The mate tags only check the replay** (section 4). Should fix. Done: `mate` and `mateIn1` to `mateIn4Plus` are reported apart as replay checks (`group` in `results.json`), and a count of trusted tags counts the 10 motif tags only. `smotheredMate` stays a motif: it is recognised from the board, not from the line's length.
7. **The label-quality claims are unsupported** (sections 1 and 2). Should fix. Done: "human-checked" is gone; section 2 says the themes come from Lichess's generator, that popularity is a vote on the puzzle, not its themes, and that the filter rests on an unverified assumption.
8. **No limit on exclusions** (section 8). Should fix. Done: more than 0.5% of the sample excluded, or every puzzle of a cell, is a failed result; exclusions are reported by cell.
9. **Disjointness not checked at run time.** Note. Done: `sample-pilot.csv` is an input, and the run stops if the two samples share an id.
10. **The rule departs from ADR 0106 without saying so** (section 9). Note. Done: section 9 names the departure and why.
11. **The sample size is set by the smoothing prior** (section 6). Note. Done: section 6 says so. The power with the smoothing term at zero is not shown: for a tag with no pilot false positive the precision scenario then has nowhere to put the false positives that make it 0.90.
12. **Puzzle rating is not player rating** (section 7). Note. Done: section 7 says so, and no result is stated per player rating.
13. **"Needs fixing" covers an unmeasured recall** (`decide()`). Note. Done: section 9 says so.
