Done: the step up now also needs its lower end above 0.01 against a 100-million-node reference on a seeded 40 games (exact on the moves where 300,000 and 1 million disagree; `branch_nodes`); 3 million left the rule (a tenth of the reference) and is not searched in the confirmatory run; the share of checked moves whose reference label changes from 30 to 100 million is reported; section 2's sentence corrected. 3. **Half the reference spend went on a step down the pilot had all but settled** (should fix). Done: the step down is read on a seeded 40 games, the step up on all of them, which buys more games for the step up within the same 12 CPU-hours (section 6).

7. **review.md was missing, and the first pilot run was undisclosed** (should fix). This file. ## Part two: the result

A second fresh agent, with the same skills, read the frozen registration, `analysis.py`, `results.json`, part one, the pilot and the post-hoc hybrid analysis, and ran nothing heavy. It found `analysis.py` and sections 0 to 10 unchanged from 3012b7ab to the results commit, section 9 applied as written (rule 1 first, both lower ends above 0.01), all 72 re-searched positions identical to the cache, and the pilot and the run on the same engine. Its verdict: the decision stands; what can be said is narrower than "300,000 gives wrong labels".

1. **The margin against the deeper reference is thin, and the effect shrinks as the reference deepens** (should fix in the write-up). On the same 40 games, the step up is 0.0252 against the 30-million-node reference and 0.0221 against the 100-million, 12% smaller (the critic's own figure, 0.0271, came from another subset; we recomputed it from the registered seed, 2,624 moves on both sides); the lower end, 0.0115, is 0.0015 above the tolerance. Done: `decision.md` gives both on the same games and says "agrees more with deeper searches", not "correct"; EXP-0028 measures the reference's convergence.
2. **The 12% must not be generalised** (minor). Done: it is stated as 12% of the 192 moves where 300,000 and 1 million disagree, the moves that decide the contrast, not a rate over all moves.
3. **The published figure showed the absolute curve** (should fix). `analysis.py` is frozen, so its `figures/disagreement.png` stays, labelled exploratory; `figures/contrasts.png`, drawn from `results.json` alone, shows the exact contrasts with their intervals and is the published one.
4. **The absolute 100,000 sits near 300,000 while the exact contrast separates them** (minor, expected). Outside the 40 step-down games, the moves where only 100,000 differs fall into the unreferenced remainder, whose 100 sampled moves each weigh about 96.5; the absolute 300,000 minus 1 million difference, 0.0246, equals the exact contrast, which confirms the code. Section 7 and the code call that remainder "concordant"; it is concordant only for 300,000 and 1 million outside the step-down games. Done: section 11 says so, and the decision notes which absolute figures rest on those 100 moves.
5. **CPU-hours over the stated plan** (minor). About 19.5 against 11.6 planned and 17.1 with the branch, every node unit under the 150% cap: 300,000 and 1 million disagreed on 7.4% of moves, against 5.3% in the pilot, giving 1,797 reference positions instead of about 1,536 and 336 deep ones instead of 258. Done: section 11 and `decision.md`.
6. **Both deciding contrasts moved outside the pilot's intervals** (minor). Step up 0.0099 in the pilot, 0.0246 here; step down 0.032 and 0.012. Same engine; most likely 20 skewed games, which also put section 6's inputs off. Done: section 11.
7. **Wording of the secondaries** (should fix). The step down is neither shown within nor shown worse (95% [0.0014, 0.023], 99% [-0.0022, 0.0267]); the decision says only that it was not read. Done.
8. **The post-hoc hybrid cannot choose a setting** (should fix). It is unregistered, scored on the same games, and swaps labels per move where a real re-search of a position would also change the neighbouring move that shares it; its upper end against 1 million, 0.0139, is above the tolerance anyway. Done: it is EXP-0028's challenger on new games, with that caveat in the decision.
9. **Records not updated** (minor). Done: the status line, `summary.md`, this part, and section 11 (including that the code's grid has 180 games and section 6's text does not; 180 is past the ceiling, so n stays 160).
10. **"Byte-identical" is narrower than it sounds** (minor). The second run read the cache, so it shows the analysis is deterministic; the engine's determinism rests on the 72 re-searches. Done: the decision says so.
11. **Scope and size** (minor). Lichess rapid games with a server analysis, rated 800 to 2199, not chess.com games; 1 million still disagrees with the reference on about 10% of moves; the gain is about 0.86 labels a player and game for 3.3 times the engine time. Done: in the decision.
