A fresh critic (an agent with the `scientific-critical-thinking` and `peer-review` skills, which had not worked on this experiment) read the pre-registration at 5b230303, `analysis.py`, `make_audit.py`, `inputs.sha256`, `HOW-TO-AUDIT.md`, the tests and the source of the generated page, without opening the key. Its 13 concerns, and what was done about each, in the commit after 63db2827. No answer existed, and the auditor had not opened the page.

1. **Section 1 contradicted the hanging-piece definition** (a back-rank mate that takes a rook, against "it still counts when the capture gives mate"). Must fix. Done: section 1 now says Rematch names every motif a line holds, so a free piece taken with mate counts, and why.
2. **Changes after registration were not logged.** Must fix. Done: section 11 lists each, with its effect on the frame and the items.
3. **The page was not bound to what the person saw.** Should fix. Done: the page's id is now the sha256 of the items, the questions, the definitions and the template; the key records the id and the page's sha256, which matches the page; `make_audit.py`'s sha256 is in section 10.
4. **The stopping rule depended on the answers.** The auditor sees his own answers and knows the allocation, so a discretionary stop between 3 and 6 blocks could follow them; and 126 items at 40 seconds never reached "past 90 minutes". Should fix. Done: the session is a fixed 6 blocks, breaks only between blocks, an early stop only with a reason written down before the next block, only whole blocks analysed (`done_items`), and the claim that a stop spends no error is gone.
5. **Blinding weaker than stated; a weak leniency check** (9 easy distractors). Should fix. Done: a false-"yes" guard on each tag's question over the 93 to 114 items with neither our tag nor its theme (no clear while its upper 97.5% bound is above 0.15), at no extra time; section 6 simulates raters with 5%, 10% and 20% false "yes" (a wrong clear at 0.80 in at most 0.0011 of the replicates at 10%); section 2 says the definitions are close to the rules and what that makes `b` measure. Not done: near-miss distractors found by the tagger's features, which would add a draw rule and items; the guard covers the gross case, and the limit is stated.
6. **The practice items primed "yes"**: all three were "tagged, not labelled" puzzles the agent's hand check had called label gaps. Should fix. Done: the practice items are pilot puzzles the hand check did not judge, one of each kind (none of our tags and no theme; our tag without the theme; both), drawn in a seeded order (`sample/practice`). Not done: choosing a "clear no" and a "borderline" one, which would need someone to judge them first.
7. **The labelled guard had little power**, and the simulation gave every tag the same labelled count. Should fix. Done: 6 labelled items for `hangingPiece` and `sacrifice` (blocks of 22, 132 items, about 4 more minutes), 3 for the others; `π·a + (1 − π)·b` of at least 0.80 is now a condition of a clear; the simulation uses the real counts and every guard. Section 4 argues the departure from "a person's verdict" for the labelled share.
8. **The definitions were hard to apply.** Should fix. Done: hanging piece names the first move and the king as a guard, and its question says "the first move"; sacrifice gives the piece values, the starting position, the settled count and the promotion exception, and drops "on purpose"; section 4's statement of the differences from Lichess now matches `rules.rs`. 9. **`HOW-TO-AUDIT.md` nudged answers.** Should fix. Done: the symmetric rule ("yes when the definition holds and no when it does not, whatever the puzzle seems to be about"), no word on how answers feed the decision, and the garbled sentence rewritten.
10. **The error claims assume a perfect rater.** Note. Done: section 6 says the claims hold for the rater modelled, and adds the lenient raters; the minimum-blocks figure is given (0.0006).
11. **A follow-up of the continuing tags only would lose the mix.** Should fix. Done: the follow-up keeps all four questions and the same mix, filling the other tags' places from their own strata.
12. **Answers can be edited or chosen among backups.** Note. Done: the page logs every answer with its time (`log` in `answers.json`), and the file that counts is the one downloaded at the end of the last sitting.
13. **Housekeeping.** Done: the sample was redrawn after the fixes from the same seeds (the frame is the same: 20,000 puzzles, none excluded); the frame's share of labelled puzzles is reported beside `π` as a diagnostic; `skewer` cannot fail, as section 6 says.
