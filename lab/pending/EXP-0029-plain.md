# EXP-0029 in plain words

**Bottom line:** running, no result yet. It asks EXP-0009's question again with its flaw fixed: three-month windows, new players, and a month's gap before the rating is read.

## What we asked

Does a fall in one of eight weaknesses over three months come before further climbing? Take a 1400 player whose early blunders fell between October–December 2025 and January–March 2026. Are they rated higher at the end of June 2026 than at the end of April, by more than a similar player whose rate didn't fall?

## Why it matters to someone learning

Rematch orders what you work on partly by how much fixing it should help. Today that order comes from research notes, not from players.

## What is being tested

EXP-0009 started its outcome from a rating built from the same games as the weakness rates, so luck in those games pulled every estimate down. Here the outcome starts at the end of April, a month later. The sizing step found 19,120 players with an outcome and about 3.6 million games, with no engine time.

## What each outcome would mean

If a weakness's fall comes before further climbing, it becomes a candidate for a new teaching order, tested in its own experiment; this one changes nothing in the product. If five named weaknesses are shown to have no link of the minimum size and the check holds, we keep the order and say on the site what was and wasn't shown. Anything else is a second "need more data": the order stays and this is the last follow-up. If no weakness is linked, the maths audit puts the clean "none" at about 9 in 10 at the planned reliabilities; if they are as low as EXP-0009's lower bounds, "need more data" becomes likeliest.

## How this helps us improve Rematch

It is the only outcome that can lead, through its own experiment, to new trainability rows. The others change a sentence on the site, not the product.

## What we still don't know

The result. And the size of the luck left after April is assumed, not measured: the audit found that for players with about 20 April games it could be twice what we registered, so the decision will report the result by how much people played in April.
